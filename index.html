Macropad

A fully custom macropad with a 0.91in OLED, 6 keyboard switches and keycaps and custom PCB; all within a 3D printed PLA matte case, held together with M3 scrwws. Funded by Hackpad, Hack Club.

Daylight

A fully custom alarm clock with a 1.1in OLED, 2 keyboard switches and keycaps, a rotary encoder, all placed in a custom PCB, controlled by the XIAO ESP32-C3. Placed in a 3D printed enclousure, and a 3D printed rotary encoder dial; held together by friction. Funded by BLARE, Hack Club.

Velo

A reimagining of the orignal gameboy. A large 3.1in colour screen and joystick. As well as 3 large buttons, all controlled by the XIAO ESP32-S3, and a small LiPo battery. All placed in a 3d printed case. Funded by Stardance YSWS, Hack Club.
